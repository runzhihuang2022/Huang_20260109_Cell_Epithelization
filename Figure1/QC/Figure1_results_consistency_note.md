# Figure 1 results-text consistency note

The recomputed section-level trajectories do not support a simple monotonic statement that epithelialization and skin-development scores increase continuously through repair and remodeling. Figure 1F shows stage-dependent, process-specific and replicate-variable trajectories. The manuscript Results and legend should use that wording unless an independently justified scoring definition is adopted and documented.

Figure 1F excludes 6 mph and 9 mph and treats the 11 registered Stereo-seq sections as the observational units. Bin-level confidence intervals from the previous plot must not be reused.
