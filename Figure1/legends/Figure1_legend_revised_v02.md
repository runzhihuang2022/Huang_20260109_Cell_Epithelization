**Figure 1. A human spatiotemporal atlas establishes the cellular taxonomy and spatial coordinates of wound epithelialization.**

**(A)** Overview of the wound epithelialization study. Stage-coded wound sequencing units comprised 9 inflammation-stage units (0–7 dpb), 22 proliferation-stage units (8–28 dpb) and 7 remodeling-stage units (1–2 mph). Four recurrent repair modes are illustrated: unhealed gradient, one-way epithelialization, almost healed, and scar/hyperplasia. In Mode 1, the morphological axis is labeled as superficial and deep. The standardized coordinate system defines horizontal epithelial advancement along the x axis and tissue depth along the y axis. Representative H&E sections are oriented to match the schematics, and the corresponding superficial/deep, newly epithelialized/front/unepithelialized, healed/focally unhealed, and scar-hyperplasia/scarless-healed locations are indicated. The footer reports 51 human sequencing units and 83 rat validation units, comprising 729,587 scRNA-seq cells, 1,031,523 Stereo-seq cell bins and 2 Visium sections.

**(B)** UMAP of 279,305 post-QC human wound cells, resolving 47 cell states across keratinocyte, skin appendage, fibroblast, immune and endothelial compartments. The UMAP and color key were regenerated directly from the H5AD object. Numbered state labels and their corresponding colors use the established annotation palette. Full state names and marker genes are provided in Table S1.

**(C)** Projection of seven epithelial and skin appendage-associated states onto four representative Stereo-seq sections from the registered 11-section human subset. All maps were generated directly from the per-section H5AD objects with equal spatial aspect. Mode-derived spatial positions are indicated above each section. Scale bars, 1 mm.

**(D)** Direct H5AD reconstruction of the 19-dpb one-way epithelialization mode, showing the unepithelialized territory, epithelialization front and newly epithelialized territory defined using the registered spatial anchors. Enlarged views show limited epithelial signal in the unepithelialized region, the advancing epithelial edge at the front, and stratified epithelial territory in the newly epithelialized region. Scale bars, 2 mm in the overview and 500 μm in the regional views. Individual-state validation maps are shown in Figure S1G.

**(E)** Spatial epithelialization module scores in four representative sections. Scores were recalculated within the exact registered 11-section subset using a shared 1st–99th percentile display range; no within-section rescaling or spatial smoothing was applied.

**(F)** Section-level temporal dynamics of epithelialization, skin development, inflammatory response, vascularization and matrix remodeling. Each open circle is one Stereo-seq section; filled circles and connecting lines show time-point means. Scores were standardized within each biological process across the 11 sections for display. Section counts were Normal, 1; 5 dpb, 1; 12 dpb, 4; 19 dpb, 2; 26 dpb, 2; and 2 mph, 1. Two-sided Kruskal–Wallis omnibus tests were performed using sections as the observational units. None of the five processes reached statistical significance. Exact statistics and P values are provided in `Figure1F_Kruskal_Wallis.csv`. *P < 0.05, **P < 0.01, ***P < 0.001; ns, not significant.

Scale bars are indicated in the panels. dpb, days post-burn; mph, months post-healing; SPTDI, superficial partial-thickness dermal injury; DPTDI, deep partial-thickness dermal injury; KC, keratinocyte; SAC, skin appendage cell; Fib, fibroblast; Endo, endothelial cell.
