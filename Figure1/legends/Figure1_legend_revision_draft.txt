Figure 1. A human spatiotemporal atlas establishes the cellular taxonomy and spatial coordinates of wound epithelialization.
(A) Overview of the wound epithelialization study. Stage-coded human wound sequencing units were distributed across inflammation (0–7 dpb, n = 9), proliferation (8–28 dpb, n = 22) and remodeling (1–2 mph, n = 7); 11 normal human units and 2 human Visium sections without encoded sampling time were not assigned to the wound-stage timeline. Samples were organized into four representative spatial repair modes: unhealed gradient, one-way epithelialization, almost healed and scar/hyperplasia. The schematics define a standardized two-dimensional wound coordinate system in which the x axis denotes horizontal epithelial advancement from intact skin or wound edge toward the wound center and the y axis denotes tissue depth from the epidermal surface into the dermal matrix. Representative H&E sections show the tissue architecture of each mode; Mode 2 and Mode 4 images were displayed after horizontal mirroring to align healed or hyperplastic tissue on the left with the schematics. The data-scale summary comprises 51 human sequencing units and 83 rat validation sequencing units, including 729,587 scRNA-seq cells, 1,031,523 Stereo-seq cell bins and 2 10x Visium sections.
(B) UMAP of 279,305 post-QC human wound single cells, resolving 47 annotated cell states across keratinocyte, skin appendage, fibroblast, immune and endothelial compartments.
(C) Spatial projection of epithelial and skin appendage-associated states across representative registered Stereo-seq sections from repair stages and injury-depth settings. Full-section insets show the location of each enlarged field.
(D) Spatial organization of the one-way epithelialization mode at 19 days post-burn. The overview defines newly epithelialized, epithelialization-front and unepithelialized territories; enlarged fields show the stratified and migratory keratinocyte states associated with each territory. Detailed individual-state maps are provided in Figure S1G.
(E) Spatial epithelialization module scores across representative registered wound sections. The score definition and display scaling are stated in Table S1 and in the panel color-bar label. High activity localizes to tissue domains undergoing epithelial coverage.
(F) Section-level temporal dynamics of epithelialization, skin-development, inflammatory-response, vascularization and matrix-remodeling programs across Normal, 5 dpb, 12 dpb, 19 dpb, 26 dpb and 2 mph sections. Points denote individual Stereo-seq sections and summary marks denote the section-level mean. No uncertainty interval is shown for time points represented by one section.
Scale bars are indicated in the panels. dpb, days post-burn; mph, months post-healing; SPTDI, superficial partial-thickness dermal injury; DPTDI, deep partial-thickness dermal injury; KC, keratinocyte; SAC, skin appendage cell; Fib, fibroblast; Endo, endothelial cell.
