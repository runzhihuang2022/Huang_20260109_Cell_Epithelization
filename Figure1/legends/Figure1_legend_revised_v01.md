**Figure 1. A human spatiotemporal atlas establishes the cellular taxonomy and spatial coordinates of wound epithelialization.**

**(A)** Overview of the wound epithelialization study. Stage-coded wound sequencing units comprised 9 inflammation-stage units (0–7 dpb), 22 proliferation-stage units (8–28 dpb) and 7 remodeling-stage units (1–2 mph). Four recurrent repair modes are illustrated: unhealed gradient, one-way epithelialization, almost healed, and scar/hyperplasia. The standardized coordinate system defines horizontal epithelial advancement along the x axis and tissue depth along the y axis. Representative H&E sections are oriented to match their schematics. The footer reports the complete registered data scale: 51 human sequencing units and 83 rat validation units, comprising 729,587 scRNA-seq cells, 1,031,523 Stereo-seq cell bins and 2 Visium sections.

**(B)** UMAP of 279,305 post-QC human wound cells, resolving 47 cell states across keratinocyte, skin appendage, fibroblast, immune and endothelial compartments. Full state names and marker genes are provided in Table S1.

**(C)** Projection of seven epithelial and skin appendage-associated states onto four representative Stereo-seq sections from the registered 11-section human subset.

**(D)** The 19-dpb one-way epithelialization mode, showing the unepithelialized territory, epithelialization front and newly epithelialized territory. Enlarged views emphasize limited epithelial signal in the unepithelialized region, migratory/transitional keratinocyte states at the front and basal-to-granular stratification in newly epithelialized tissue. Individual-state validation maps are shown in Figure S1G.

**(E)** Spatial epithelialization module scores in four representative sections. Scores were recalculated within the exact registered 11-section subset using a shared 1st–99th percentile display range; no within-section rescaling or spatial smoothing was applied.

**(F)** Section-level temporal dynamics of epithelialization, skin development, inflammatory response, vascularization and matrix remodeling. Each open circle is one Stereo-seq section; filled circles and connecting lines show time-point means. Scores were standardized within each biological process across the 11 sections for display. Section counts were Normal, 1; 5 dpb, 1; 12 dpb, 4; 19 dpb, 2; 26 dpb, 2; and 2 mph, 1. The trajectories are stage-dependent and non-synchronous rather than uniformly monotonic.

Scale bars are indicated in the panels. dpb, days post-burn; mph, months post-healing; SPTDI, superficial partial-thickness dermal injury; DPTDI, deep partial-thickness dermal injury; KC, keratinocyte; SAC, skin appendage cell; Fib, fibroblast; Endo, endothelial cell.
