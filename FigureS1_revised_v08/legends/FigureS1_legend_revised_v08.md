**Figure S1. Clinical wound modes, sample-level sequencing quality, burn-depth-dependent outcomes, cell annotation and spatial controls supporting Figure 1.**

**(A)** Vertically arranged representative clinical appearances of the four lower-leg wound-healing modes defined in Figure 1A: unhealed gradient, one-way epithelialization, almost healed, and scar/hyperplasia. Approximate 2-cm scale bars were inferred from visible adult lower-leg anatomy and are not acquisition-calibrated measurements.

**(B)** Per-sample distributions of the Number of UMI and Number of Gene for all human molecular datasets, shown in vertically arranged modality rows for scRNA-seq (38 samples; 279,305 post-QC cells), Stereo-seq (11 registered sections; 387,061 cell bins), and 10x Visium (2 sections; 4,449 spots). Values are shown as log10(count + 1). Boxes indicate the interquartile range with the median; whiskers extend to 1.5 times the interquartile range. Plot codes R01-R38, ST01-ST11, and V01-V02 are defined in `data/FigureS1B_sample_code_mapping.csv`.

**(C)** Illustration of the final healing outcomes after superficial and deep partial-thickness dermal burns, together with 5-dpb H&E sections and the legacy residual-appendage quantification. SPTDI preserves abundant residual cutaneous appendages and closes at approximately 2 weeks with scarless healing. DPTDI leaves few residual appendages and closes at approximately 4 weeks with scar formation. The outcome illustrations are explanatory and are not drawn to scale. The 1-mm H&E scale bar is retained from the 600-dpi source. The statistical graphic is reproduced without resampling from the previous Figure S31A; the exact statistical test was not specified in the supplied legacy legend and remains to be reconciled before final submission.

**(D)** Heat maps of mean canonical-marker expression z-scores for all 47 annotated cell states. Epithelial/appendage, stromal/vascular, and immune states are displayed in three independent blocks, with the corresponding canonical marker genes shown directly as heat-map column labels. Dedicated inter-block label space prevents cell-state and marker-gene labels from overlapping neighboring heat maps.

**(E)** Spatial quality-control maps of Number of UMI and Number of Gene for all 11 registered human Stereo-seq sections. Sections are arranged in a compact five-column matrix, with paired UMI and Gene maps shown at identical dimensions. Values are log10-transformed and displayed with two shared, metric-specific color scales. Sections use the registered sample-specific rotations without additional mirroring or axis inversion. Scale bars, 1 mm.

**(F)** Temporal spatial maps showing KC_Basal, KC_Basal_Mig, KC_Spinous, KC_Spinous_Mig, KC_Spinous_Mat, and KC_Granular across normal skin, 5 dpb, 12-dpb SPTDI, 19 dpb, and 2 mph Stereo-seq sections. The five sections are arranged chronologically in one row across the full A4 page width. Colors follow the supplied 47-cell-state legend; light-gray points indicate other annotated cell states. Map dimensions match those used in panel E, and all sections use the registered rotations. Scale bars, 1 mm.

Abbreviations: DPTDI, deep partial-thickness dermal injury; dpb, days post burn; KC, keratinocyte; mph, months post healing; SPTDI, superficial partial-thickness dermal injury; UMI, unique molecular identifier.
