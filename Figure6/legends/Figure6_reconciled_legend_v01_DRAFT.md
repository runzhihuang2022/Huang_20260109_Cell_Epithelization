(F) Krt14-CreER;Pdgfra-DreER dual-recombinase intersectional tracing strategy and adult wound evidence. **Panel source unresolved: the construct map, original control and wound images, Z-stacks, animal-level quantification and exact statistical result are required before this panel can be described as experimental evidence.**

(G) Evidence-strength-coded model of injury-associated Fib_K14 epithelial-like plasticity at the Epi-Front. Solid connectors denote perturbation-supported relationships, whereas dashed connectors denote inferred or associated relationships. Fib_K14 cells retain fibroblast-lineage markers while acquiring epithelial-associated markers and are spatially associated with re-epithelialization. The model does not establish complete fibroblast-to-keratinocyte conversion, direct epidermal incorporation or a functional requirement for wound closure.

Scale bars are indicated in the panels. Epi-Front, epithelialization front; Fib_K14, KRT14-positive fibroblast subpopulation; IGFL1, IGF-like family member 1; n.d., not detected. Statistical notation cannot be finalized until the source tables and exact observational units are supplied.
