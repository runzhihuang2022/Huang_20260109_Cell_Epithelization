# Figure S2. Cell-state-resolved spatial trajectories and statistical validation of epithelialization-front enrichment supporting Figure 2.

**(A)** Cell-state-resolved spatial trajectory atlas for 44 supplementary cell states across six representative human wound Stereo-seq sections. Each row shows one cell state. SAC_SG_Dark, SAC_SG_Progenitor and KC_Spinous_Mig are excluded because they are displayed individually in Figure 2A. Un-epi, Epi-Front and Newly-epi are labeled at a 30° angle in the first row of every section. Sample-specific coordinates were reconstructed from the registered spatial-anchor JSON files and checked against the corresponding boundary-mask files. Colors within each spatial map indicate local bin density; row-label colors indicate the broad cellular compartment.

**(B)** Cell-state-resolved temporal trajectory analysis aligned row by row with panel A. For every cell state, the left sparkline shows the section-median horizontal position relative to the sample-specific spatial reference and the right sparkline shows the section-median epidermis-to-dermis depth across 5, 12, 19 and 26 days post burn and 2 months post healing. Line colors follow the fixed 47-cell-state color registry. Analyses include all 10 registered wound Stereo-seq sections. The rightmost column gives the total number of annotated bins across these sections. Two-sided Mann-Whitney U tests compare every available pair of time points for each cell state and axis; P values were adjusted across all 834 comparisons using the Benjamini-Hochberg method. No comparison remained significant at FDR q < 0.05, so no `ns` labels or significance symbols are displayed. The pairwise results are provided in `FigureS2B_timepoint_pairwise_statistics.csv`; overall Kruskal-Wallis and Spearman trend statistics remain available in `FigureS2B_per_cell_statistics.csv`.

**(C)** Regional enrichment heat map for all annotated cell subpopulations in the 19 dpb_p1 Stereo-seq section across Un-epi, Epi-Front and Newly-epi regions. Values are log2 enrichment relative to the section-wide cell-state proportion. Stars denote Benjamini-Hochberg-adjusted two-sided Fisher exact-test significance for each cell-state-by-region comparison. Cell states with positive and significant Epi-Front enrichment are labeled in red.

**(D)** Regional enrichment plots for KC_Spinous_Mig, SAC_SG_Progenitor and Fib_K14 in the 19 dpb_p1 section. Bars show log2 enrichment in Un-epi, Epi-Front and Newly-epi regions. Brackets compare Epi-Front with Un-epi and Newly-epi using two-sided Fisher exact tests on cell-bin counts, with Benjamini-Hochberg correction across the six pairwise comparisons.

Statistical units are Stereo-seq sections in panel B and cell bins in panels C and D. *q < 0.05, **q < 0.01, ***q < 0.001. KC, keratinocyte; SAC, skin appendage cell; dpb, days post burn; mph, months post healing.
