Figure S7. Chromatin-accessibility quality control and cross-omics evidence supporting the epithelial-like Fib_K14 state.

(A) RNA-anchored label-transfer projection of scATAC-seq cells, shown by transferred cell-state labels and Klf4 gene score. The supplied assets did not contain a joint-coordinate RNA–ATAC co-embedding object; therefore, this panel is described as an RNA-anchored scATAC projection rather than a true co-embedding.

(B) scATAC-seq quality-control summaries for predicted Fib_K14 cells and other predicted fibroblast states. TSS enrichment and nucleosome ratio were comparable between groups. Fib_K14 cells had slightly fewer fragments and lower doublet scores; medians and Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted P values are shown where significant.

(C) ArchR genome-browser tracks at Krt14, Krt5 and Tacstd2 across predicted fibroblast states. These source-reported tracks visualize epithelial-associated accessibility in Fib_K14-like states; no inferential P values are assigned to the track images.

(D) Audit of KLF4 motif and footprinting evidence. No KLF4 motif match was present in the supplied peak-annotation table, preventing a valid KLF4 footprinting analysis. This panel records the negative audit result and is not interpreted as evidence for KLF4 motif enrichment.

(E) Cross-omics state-axis analysis relating rank-aligned Klf4 ATAC gene-score pseudobulks to TACSTD2 RNA pseudobulks. The association was positive (Pearson r = 0.53, P = 0.0037, BH-adjusted q = 0.0183; n = 28 rank-aligned pseudobulks). Because RNA and ATAC values were rank-aligned rather than donor-paired, the result supports state-axis concordance but not a direct or causal chromatin mechanism. Direct single-cell ATAC correlations between Klf4 and the prespecified epithelial genes Krt14, Krt5, Pdgfra, Tp63 and Tacstd2 were not significant after BH correction (all q ≥ 0.40).

Fib_K14 marker accessibility was strongly elevated relative to other predicted fibroblast states (Cliff’s delta = 0.79, BH-adjusted q = 5.6 × 10^-190). In contrast, Klf4 gene score was slightly lower in predicted Fib_K14 cells (Cliff’s delta = -0.057, BH-adjusted q = 0.049). These results support a coordinated epithelial-like accessibility/expression state but do not establish direct KLF4 binding, chromatin remodeling, fate conversion or pluripotency.
