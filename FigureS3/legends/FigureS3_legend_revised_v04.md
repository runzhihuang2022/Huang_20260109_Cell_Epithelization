**Figure S3. Time-resolved Fib_K14-centered spatial co-occurrence with keratinocyte and skin-appendage states.**

**(A)** Time-resolved TOP10 Fib_K14-conditioned co-occurrence scores for all keratinocyte (KC) and skin-appendage (SAC) states at Normal, 5 dpb, 12 dpb, 19 dpb, 26 dpb and 2 mph. Colored cells show the time-point mean score and within-time rank after the number sign; gray cells indicate states outside that time point’s TOP10. Time-point means were calculated across the registered Stereo-seq sections (n = 1, 1, 4, 2, 2 and 1 sections, respectively). These rankings are descriptive and do not by themselves denote statistical significance.

**(B)** Temporal Fib_K14-conditioned co-occurrence scores for Fib_K14 itself, KC_Spinous_Mig and SAC_SG_Progenitor. Lines show time-point means and points show individual Stereo-seq sections. The dashed line indicates the null ratio of 1. The lower matrix reports Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted time-point permutation P values. Fib_K14 self-co-occurrence was significant at every time point (q = 0.017), whereas neither KC_Spinous_Mig nor SAC_SG_Progenitor showed significant positive co-occurrence after correction. Fib_K14 self-co-occurrence measures spatial clustering of the conditioned state and is not a cross-cell-state co-localization test.

**(C)** Distance-dependent co-occurrence probability ratios in the 26 dpb_p2 section, conditioned on Fib_K14, together with the ten highest closest-distance scores when the Fib_K14 self-reference is retained. Fib_K14 and KC_Spinous_Mig are highlighted in red and dark blue, respectively; other states are shown in gray. KC_Spinous_Mig had the highest closest-distance ratio (2.612), while the Fib_K14 self-reference ranked ninth (2.162). In the prespecified fixed 50-µm analysis, Fib_K14 self-co-occurrence was significant (score = 3.154, P = 0.001, q = 0.017), whereas the elevated KC_Spinous_Mig score did not remain significant after correction across Fib_K14 and all KC/SAC states (score = 2.845, P = 0.021, q = 0.179). Distances were converted using 0.5 µm per spatial coordinate unit.

For panels B and C, the conditional co-occurrence score is the probability of observing the neighboring state around Fib_K14 divided by its marginal spatial probability; values greater than 1 indicate enrichment. Statistical significance was evaluated using 999 spatial-label permutations with coordinates and cell-state counts held fixed. Time-point P values compared the observed mean across registered sections with the corresponding mean permutation distribution and were adjusted across 17 tested states and six time points (102 tests) using the Benjamini–Hochberg procedure. dpb, days post burn; mph, months post healing; ns, not significant.
