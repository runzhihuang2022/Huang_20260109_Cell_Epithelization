**Figure S3. Time-resolved Fib_K14-centered spatial co-occurrence with keratinocyte and skin-appendage states.**

**(A)** Time-resolved TOP10 Fib_K14-conditioned co-occurrence scores for all keratinocyte (KC) and skin-appendage (SAC) states in Normal, 5 dpb, 12 dpb, 19 dpb, 26 dpb and 2 mph sections. Colored cells show the time-point mean score and within-time rank after the number sign; gray cells indicate states outside that time point's TOP10. Time-point means were calculated across the registered Stereo-seq sections (n = 1, 1, 4, 2, 2 and 1 sections, respectively). The rankings are descriptive and do not by themselves denote statistical significance.

**(B)** Upper, temporal cross-cell-state co-occurrence scores between Fib_K14 and KC_Spinous_Mig or SAC_SG_Progenitor. Lower, Fib_K14 self-spatial aggregation shown separately from the cross-cell-state analysis. Lines indicate time-point means, points indicate individual Stereo-seq sections, and the dashed horizontal line denotes a co-occurrence ratio of 1. Time-group differences were tested at the section level using the Kruskal-Wallis test. None of the three temporal comparisons was significant; therefore, non-significant P values are not displayed in the figure. Exact test results are provided in the accompanying source-data table.

**(C)** Code-reproduced spatial co-occurrence panel for the 26 dpb_p2 section, conditioned on Fib_K14. The left plot shows the complete distance-dependent co-occurrence probability curves using the original cell-state colors; Fib_K14 and the ten highest cross-cell-state curves are emphasized, whereas other states are displayed with reduced opacity. The right plot shows the ten highest closest-distance cross-cell-state scores after excluding the Fib_K14 self-reference. KC_Spinous_Mig had the highest closest-distance score (2.612). The full cell-state legend is shown beneath the plots.

For all panels, the conditional co-occurrence score is the probability of observing the indicated state around Fib_K14 divided by its marginal spatial probability; values greater than 1 indicate enrichment. dpb, days post burn; KC, keratinocyte; mph, months post healing; SAC, skin appendage cell.
