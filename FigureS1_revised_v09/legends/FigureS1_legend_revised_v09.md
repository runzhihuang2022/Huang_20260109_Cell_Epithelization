**Figure S1. Clinical wound modes, sample-level sequencing quality, burn-depth-dependent outcomes, cell annotation and spatial controls supporting Figure 1.**

**(A)** Vertically arranged representative clinical appearances of the four lower-leg wound-healing modes defined in Figure 1A: unhealed gradient, one-way epithelialization, almost healed, and scar/hyperplasia. Approximate 2-cm scale bars were inferred from visible adult lower-leg anatomy and are not acquisition-calibrated measurements.

**(B)** Per-sample distributions of the Number of UMI and Number of Gene for all human molecular datasets, shown in vertically arranged modality rows for scRNA-seq (38 samples; 279,305 post-QC cells), Stereo-seq (11 registered sections; 387,061 cell bins), and 10x Visium (2 sections; 4,449 spots). Values are shown as log10(count + 1). Boxes indicate the interquartile range with the median; whiskers extend to 1.5 times the interquartile range. Plot codes R01-R38, ST01-ST11, and V01-V02 are defined in the sample-code mapping table.

**(C)** Illustration of the final healing outcomes after superficial and deep partial-thickness dermal burns, together with 5-dpb H&E sections and the legacy residual-appendage quantification. SPTDI preserves abundant residual cutaneous appendages and closes at approximately 2 weeks with scarless healing. DPTDI leaves few residual appendages and closes at approximately 4 weeks with scar formation. The outcome illustrations are explanatory and are not drawn to scale. The 1-mm H&E scale bar is retained from the source. The exact statistical test used for the legacy residual-appendage graphic remains to be reconciled before final submission.

**(D)** Heat maps of mean canonical-marker expression z-scores for all 47 annotated cell states. Epithelial/appendage, stromal/vascular, and immune states are displayed in three independent blocks, with the corresponding canonical marker genes shown directly as heat-map column labels.

**(E)** Spatial quality-control feature plots of Number of UMI and Number of Gene for all 11 registered human Stereo-seq sections. Paired UMI and Gene maps are displayed in equal 35.6 × 8.0-mm plot boxes. Values are log10-transformed and shown using shared metric-specific color scales positioned outside the maps at the lower-right of the panel. Sections use the registered sample-specific rotations without additional mirroring or axis inversion. Scale bars, 1 mm.

**(F)** Temporal spatial maps showing KC_Basal, KC_Basal_Mig, KC_Spinous, KC_Spinous_Mig, KC_Spinous_Mat, and KC_Granular across normal skin, 5 dpb, 12-dpb SPTDI, 19 dpb, and 2 mph Stereo-seq sections. The five sections are arranged chronologically in one row. All maps use the same 35.6 × 8.0-mm plot boxes as panel E. Colored keratinocyte points are displayed at reduced size to prevent overlap; light-gray points indicate other annotated cell states. The categorical legend is placed outside the spatial maps at the lower-right of the panel. Sections use the registered rotations. Scale bars, 1 mm.

Abbreviations: DPTDI, deep partial-thickness dermal injury; dpb, days post burn; KC, keratinocyte; mph, months post healing; SPTDI, superficial partial-thickness dermal injury; UMI, unique molecular identifier.
