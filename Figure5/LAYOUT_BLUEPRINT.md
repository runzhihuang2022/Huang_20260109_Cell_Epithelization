# A4 layout blueprint

## Figure 5

Preferred orientation: A4 landscape, 297 × 210 mm, with 10 mm outer margins and 3–4 mm inter-panel gaps.

```text
┌──────────────────── A (maps + NN) ────────────────────┬──── B (LR) ────┐
├──────────── C (gradients + receptor) ──────────────────┼──── D (maps) ──┤
├──────────────── E (19/26 dpb IF, matched crops) ──────┴─────────────────┤
├──────── F (qPCR) ───────┬──────── G (IF + quant) ─────┬──── H (morph) ─┤
└───────────────────────── I (integrated model) ─────────────────────────┘
```

Panel I should be shallow and horizontal. Microscopy and its quantification stay adjacent. No figure-wide title is placed inside the artboard.

## Figure S5

Required orientation: A4 portrait, 210 × 297 mm. Use a three-column modular grid, except microscopy rows may span two columns.

```text
row 1: A | B | C
row 2: D (two columns) | E
row 3: F | G | H
row 4: I | J (two columns)
row 5: K | L | M
```

All panels are fitted at final print size before font QC. Similar plot types share axis-box dimensions; legends sit in reserved whitespace rather than over data.
