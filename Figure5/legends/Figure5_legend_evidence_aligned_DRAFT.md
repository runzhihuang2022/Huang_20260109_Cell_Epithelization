**Figure 5. IGFL1-associated signaling in the epithelialization-front microenvironment and Fib_K14 epithelial-like plasticity.**

**(A)** Spatial distributions of KC_Spinous_Mig and Fib_K14 and section-level nearest-neighbor analysis across Un-epi, Epi-Front and Newly-epi territories at 19 dpb. The reproducible permutation analysis did not detect shorter-than-random distances in any territory (FDR-adjusted P = 1.0 for both registered 19-dpb sections).

**(B)** Candidate ligand–receptor enrichment between Epi-Front-associated epithelial states and Fib_K14. IGFL1–IGFLR1 was nominated by the permutation-based score (FDR-adjusted P = 0.01098); this computational association does not establish direct ligand–receptor engagement.

**(C)** IGFL1 expression along registered wound coordinates and IGFLR1 expression in Fib_K14. The KC_Spinous_Mig wound-x association was statistically significant but small (Spearman r = 0.034, FDR-adjusted P = 0.00696), whereas its surface-axis association was not significant (r = -0.0125, P = 0.305). IGFLR1 expression was higher in Fib_K14 than in other fibroblast states in the bin-level comparison (FDR-adjusted P = 5.38 × 10^-22).

**(D)** Spatial feature maps of IGFL1 and IGFLR1 and a descriptive marker-defined adjacency overlay. Aggregated IGFL1–IGFLR1 spatial correlation was not significant (r = -0.0562, permutation P = 0.7026).
