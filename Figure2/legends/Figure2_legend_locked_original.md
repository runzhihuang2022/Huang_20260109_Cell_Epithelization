# Figure 2. Spatial geometric modeling identifies the epithelialization front as a repair niche enriched with KC_Spinous_Mig, SAC_SG_Progenitor and Fib_K14.

(A) Spatial trajectory analysis of SAC_SG_Dark, SAC_SG_Progenitor and KC_Spinous_Mig across representative wound-healing stages and injury-depth settings. Projection onto the x-y wound coordinate system shows that appendage-associated progenitor states move upward from deeper dermal regions, whereas KC_Spinous_Mig localizes along the horizontal epithelialization axis near the advancing wound edge.

(B) Regional enrichment analysis at 19 dpb comparing Un-epi, Newly-epi and Epi-Front regions. KC_Spinous_Mig and Fib_K14 show preferential enrichment in the Epi-Front, indicating that this region contains a specialized cellular composition rather than a simple anatomical boundary.

(C) Three-dimensional trajectory modeling of Epi-Front-associated cell states across healing time, distance to front and epidermis-dermis depth. KC_Spinous_Mig follows a horizontal migration path toward the unepithelialized area, whereas SAC_SG_Progenitor and Fib_K14 converge upward toward the epithelialization front.

(D) Representative spatial maps showing co-localization of Fib_K14 with KC_Spinous_Mig, SAC_SG_Progenitor and related epithelial states at 12 dpb and 19 dpb. The increased spatial convergence at 19 dpb supports coordinated accumulation of these cell states during active epithelialization.

(E) Quantification of Fib_K14 proportions across Un-epi, Epi-Front and Newly-epi regions. Fib_K14 is significantly enriched in the Epi-Front, supporting its association with the spatial niche of active epithelial advancement.

(F) Multiplex immunofluorescence staining of temporal human wound specimens validating epithelialization-front-associated programs. SCGAB2B/KRT17/S100A7 staining marks SAC_SG_Progenitor-associated cells, whereas SOX9/MMP3/KRT6B staining marks KC_Spinous_Mig-associated cells. Quantification shows progressive accumulation of these marker-positive cells during the proliferative epithelialization window.

Scale bars are indicated in the panels. dpb, days post-burn; dpi, days post-injury; SPTDI, superficial partial-thickness dermal injury; DPTDI, deep partial-thickness dermal injury; Un-epi, unepithelialized region; Epi-Front, epithelialization-front region; Newly-epi, newly epithelialized region.

# Figure S2. Statistical validation of epithelialization-front enrichment, spatial co-localization and baseline staining controls supporting Figure 2.

(A) Spatial enrichment score heat map for all annotated cell subpopulations across Un-epi, Epi-Front and Newly-epi regions at 19 dpb, with statistically enriched cell states marked by significance stars.

(B) Bar plots showing enrichment scores for KC_Spinous_Mig, SAC_SG_Progenitor and Fib_K14 across Un-epi, Epi-Front and Newly-epi regions. Significance stars indicate enrichment in the Epi-Front compared with the other two spatial regions.

(C) Pearson correlation scatter plot of spot-level abundance between Fib_K14 and KC_Spinous_Mig across epithelialization-front-containing spatial transcriptomic sections, with significance indicated by correlation P value.

(D) Spearman correlation scatter plot of spot-level abundance between Fib_K14 and SAC_SG_Progenitor across epithelialization-front-containing spatial transcriptomic sections, with significance indicated by correlation P value.

(E) Spatial nearest-neighbor distance analysis quantifying physical proximity among Fib_K14, KC_Spinous_Mig and SAC_SG_Progenitor in representative wound sections. Significance stars indicate shorter observed distances than randomized spatial permutations.

(F) Multiplex immunofluorescence staining of healthy skin showing baseline expression of Fib_K14-associated and epithelialization-front-associated markers.

(G) Quantification of marker-positive cells in healthy skin and wound epithelialization-front specimens. Significance stars indicate increased marker-positive cell proportions in wound epithelialization-front regions compared with healthy skin.

Data are shown as mean +/- s.e.m. unless otherwise indicated. Statistical significance was determined using two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test for regional enrichment comparisons, Pearson or Spearman correlation test for spatial co-abundance analyses, and permutation testing for nearest-neighbor distance analysis. *P < 0.05, **P < 0.01, ***P < 0.001; ns, not significant.
