# Figure 2. Spatial geometric modeling identifies the epithelialization front as a repair niche containing KC_Spinous_Mig and Fib_K14.

(A) Spatial trajectory analysis of SAC_SG_Dark, SAC_SG_Progenitor and KC_Spinous_Mig across representative wound-healing stages and injury-depth settings. Projection onto the standardized x-y wound coordinate system visualizes the horizontal and vertical distributions of these epithelial and skin-appendage-associated states.

(B) Regional enrichment analysis in the 19 dpb_p1 Stereo-seq section comparing unepithelialized (Un-epi), epithelialization-front (Epi-Front) and newly epithelialized (Newly-epi) regions. KC_Spinous_Mig and Fib_K14 show positive enrichment at the Epi-Front; SAC_SG_Progenitor does not show Epi-Front enrichment in this categorical-label analysis.

(C) Orthogonal projections of trajectory centroids across healing time, distance to the epithelialization front and epidermis-to-dermis depth for KC_Spinous_Mig, SAC_SG_Progenitor and Fib_K14. The three matched projections replace the previous perspective rendering while retaining the prespecified geometric dimensions.

(D) Representative spatial maps showing the distributions of Fib_K14, KC_Spinous_Mig, SAC_SG_Progenitor and related epithelial states at 12 dpb and 19 dpb. These maps describe spatial co-distribution but do not by themselves establish cellular co-localization.

(E) Proportions of Stereo-seq bins assigned as Fib_K14 in Un-epi, Epi-Front and Newly-epi regions of the same 19 dpb_p1 section used in panel B. Pairwise two-sided Fisher exact tests with Benjamini-Hochberg correction show a higher Fib_K14 proportion at the Epi-Front than in either comparison region.

(F) Multiplex immunofluorescence staining of temporal human wound specimens. KRT77, SCGB2A2 and S100A7 mark the first staining series, and SOX9, MMP3 and KRT6B mark the second series. The corresponding quantification plots are enlarged and placed adjacent to their matched micrographs. Protein labels are set in roman type; scale bars are indicated in the images.

dpb, days post-burn; dpi, days post-injury; SPTDI, superficial partial-thickness dermal injury; DPTDI, deep partial-thickness dermal injury; Un-epi, unepithelialized region; Epi-Front, epithelialization-front region; Newly-epi, newly epithelialized region.

# Figure S2. Statistical assessment of regional enrichment and spatial association supporting Figure 2.

(A) Heat map of log2 regional enrichment for all annotated cell states across Un-epi, Epi-Front and Newly-epi regions in the 19 dpb_p1 Stereo-seq section. Enrichment is calculated from categorical bin labels relative to all other regions.

(B) Regional log2 enrichment for KC_Spinous_Mig, SAC_SG_Progenitor and Fib_K14. KC_Spinous_Mig and Fib_K14 are positively enriched at the Epi-Front, whereas SAC_SG_Progenitor is not.

(C) Epi-Front bin-level Pearson correlation between Fib_K14 and KC_Spinous_Mig mapping scores (r = -0.268, P = 7.85 x 10^-158; n = 9,595 bins).

(D) Epi-Front bin-level Spearman correlation between Fib_K14 and SAC_SG_Progenitor mapping scores (rho = -0.024, P = 0.019; n = 9,595 bins).

(E) Categorical-label nearest-neighbor distances among Fib_K14, KC_Spinous_Mig and SAC_SG_Progenitor compared with label-permuted expectations. In this implementation, observed distances are longer than randomized expectations and therefore do not support preferential proximity.

(F) Multiplex immunofluorescence controls from matched 0-day baseline skin (S-0d and D-0d) showing KRT77/SCGB2A2/S100A7 and SOX9/MMP3/KRT6B merged fields. Experimental pixels were retained; only cropping and page layout were applied.

(G) Prespecified comparison of marker-positive cells between healthy skin and wound Epi-Front specimens. Raw counts, biological replicate identifiers and denominators were not located; this panel remains explicitly non-submission-ready and no inferential test is reported.

For panels A, B and E, Stereo-seq bins are analytical units and are not treated as independent biological replicates. Regional Fib_K14 comparisons in Figure 2E use two-sided Fisher exact tests with Benjamini-Hochberg correction. Panels C and D use the stated two-sided correlation tests. Panel E uses label-permutation reference distributions. Exact results are reported in the panel and source-data tables; no mean +/- s.e.m. claim is applied to single-section bin summaries.
