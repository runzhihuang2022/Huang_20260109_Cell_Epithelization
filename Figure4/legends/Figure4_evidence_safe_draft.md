# Figure 4. KLF4 is associated with an epithelial-like program in Fib_K14 cells and promotes keratin-associated features after overexpression.

(A) Evidence summary and spatial distribution of KLF4 in Fib_K14 cells at 19 dpb. Wound-axis analyses use the registered 19 dpb Stereo-seq sections and the standardized unepithelialized-to-newly epithelialized coordinate.

(B) Spatial expression of KLF4, POU5F1, SOX2 and MYC. These panels show expression and do not by themselves establish pluripotency or cell-fate conversion.

(C) KLF4 expression and a predefined KLF4 target-gene proxy in Fib_K14 versus other fibroblasts. The proxy is not a substitute for SCENIC regulon AUC.

(D) Multiplex immunofluorescence of human wound tissue showing KLF4, KRT14, VIM and DAPI, with source quantification of nuclear KLF4 signal and KLF4-positive cells.

(E) Time-lapse phase-contrast imaging of control and KLF4-overexpressing fibroblasts with source-derived cell-length and length-to-width measurements.

(F) Immunofluorescence analysis of keratin-associated protein induction in GFP-positive KLF4-overexpressing fibroblasts. The final keratin label requires reconciliation with the source AI artwork before publication.

(G) Reserved for source-generated RNA-seq differential-expression and GSEA panels; not shown in the evidence-safe draft because the raw result tables were not located.

(H) Rat scATAC-seq RNA-label transfer, Klf4 gene-score projection and accessibility browser tracks at Krt14 and Krt5. Motif enrichment and footprinting are not shown because the available motif workflow did not produce KLF4 motif hits.

(I) Evidence-bounded working model. KLF4 overexpression is linked to altered fibroblast morphology and keratin-associated features; the upstream signal and complete fate relationship remain unresolved.

Data are shown as in the source analyses. Exact n values, statistical tests and multiple-testing procedures must be carried from the primary source tables for each experimental comparison. Gene symbols are italicized where they denote genes; protein labels remain roman.
